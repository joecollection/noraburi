    Wellness Tourism Boom Driving Premium Stays. C9 Hotelworks identifies wellness as Koh Samui's highest-growth structural demand driver — characterised by year-round visitation and longer stays averaging 4–5 nights, insulating resort revenues from peak-season volatility.
  • 3
    Strict Development Restrictions Preserve Asset Value. Koh Samui enforces building height limits and coastal development restrictions. With hotel supply growing at just 1% CAGR, existing beachfront assets represent a near-irreplaceable landbank.

    Cruise + Long-Haul Diversification. 50 cruise ships in 2024 (nearly doubling YoY) and European long-haul at 56% of arrivals provides counter-cyclical insulation from Asian source market volatility.

    MICE & Event Demand Activator. Thailand's MICE strategy targets Koh Samui as a premium island venue. Resort's event spaces and villa product align with high-ADR group contracting, unlocking a revenue layer absent from current operations.

ADR ramp to ฿6,800–7,500 as brand repositioning takes effect. Occupancy builds to 75–80%. NOI grows from ฿110M to ฿140–175M. OTA rankings and review scores consolidate in Top 5 Chaweng.
